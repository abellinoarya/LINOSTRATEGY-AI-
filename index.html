      The fund's mandate

      Linostrategy Investment Management is a privately held investment vehicle pursuing long-term capital appreciation through a concentrated core allocation to digital assets, complemented by selective, high-conviction positions in Indonesian equities. The fund is managed with a multi-decade horizon, low turnover, and a conviction-weighted approach to position sizing.

      • Digital assetsBitcoin held as a long-term store of value and the fund's strategic core.
      • Indonesian equitiesSelective exposure to technology platforms, investment holdings and banking.
      • ApproachConviction-weighted, low-turnover, long-term oriented.

      Fund history

      From inception in November 2017 to present.

      2017
      Fund inception

      Linostrategy Investment Management is founded in November 2017 with an initial capital of Rp 100 million. First investment made in PT Betonjaya Manunggal Tbk (BTON), a steel wire rod manufacturer listed on the IDX.

      2019
      Energy sector entry

      The fund initiates a position in PT Harum Energy Tbk (HRUM), a coal mining and energy company, broadening the portfolio into Indonesia's natural resources sector.

      2021
      Significant expansion — five new positions

      The fund's most active year. New positions opened across five Indonesian-listed companies:

      • PT Astra Otoparts Tbk (AUTO) — automotive components
      • PT Map Aktif Adiperkasa Tbk (MAPA) — sports & lifestyle retail
      • PT Bank Danamon Indonesia Tbk (BDMN) — banking
      • PT Cahaya Aero Services Tbk (CASS) — aviation services
      • PT Pan Brothers Tbk (PBRX) — garment & textile manufacturing
      2023
      Digital assets & conviction re-allocation

      The fund pivots toward a high-conviction concentrated strategy. New positions initiated in Bitcoin (BTC) as a long-term store of value, alongside PT Saratoga Investama Sedaya Tbk (SRTG) and PT GoTo Gojek Tokopedia Tbk (GOTO). These three positions form the fund's current core holdings.

      Today
      Current portfolio

      The fund continues to hold BTC, SRTG and GOTO as its concentrated core, managed with a multi-decade horizon and low turnover.
